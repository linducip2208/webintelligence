# The Investigation Lifecycle

> Search to monitor: every stage of the pipeline, clickable into its guide.

Click any stage to open its guide.

```text
SEARCH → TARGET → SCOPE → COLLECT → NORMALIZE → ENTITY RESOLUTION → GRAPH
  → CORRELATE → RISK → FINDINGS → EVIDENCE → CASE → REPORT → MONITOR
```

### Stage 1 — [Search](/docs/investigations/target-types)

Find the starting point: a domain, IP, email or keyword.

### Stage 2 — [Target](/docs/investigations/target-types)

Validate the target and pick its type.

### Stage 3 — [Scope](/docs/investigations/scope)

Choose what to collect: DNS, TLS, tech, content and more.

### Stage 4 — [Collect](/docs/investigations/collection)

Queued to completed — every state is visible.

### Stage 5 — [Normalize](/docs/discovery/collectors)

Raw captures become clean, comparable records.

### Stage 6 — [Entity Resolution](/docs/intelligence/entity-resolution)

Mentions become confident identities.

### Stage 7 — [Graph](/docs/intelligence/graph)

Relationships you can traverse and pivot.

### Stage 8 — [Correlate](/docs/intelligence/pivoting)

Shared infrastructure becomes findings.

### Stage 9 — [Risk](/docs/intelligence/risk)

Explainable 0-100 scores with evidence.

### Stage 10 — [Findings](/docs/intelligence/findings)

Triage what correlation surfaced.

### Stage 11 — [Evidence](/docs/evidence/evidence)

Preserve sources with provenance.

### Stage 12 — [Case](/docs/evidence/cases)

Bundle notes, tasks, members and links.

### Stage 13 — [Report](/docs/evidence/reports)

Export web, PDF, CSV, JSON, XLSX or Markdown.

### Stage 14 — [Monitor](/docs/monitoring/watchlists)

Watchlists, alerts and workflows keep watching.
